FAQs

Common questions

How much space do I need?

Final dimensions will be confirmed once the setup is measured. When enquiring, send through the available width, depth and ceiling height so we can confirm if your space is suitable.

Can PortaGolf be set up indoors or outdoors?

Yes, subject to space, surface, weather and access conditions. Outdoor bookings may depend on suitable cover and safe weather conditions.

Do guests need golf experience?

No. PortaGolf is designed to be easy for beginners while still being fun for people who already play golf.

Do you supply the equipment?

Yes. The simulator setup, clubs, balls and basic playing equipment are included unless otherwise agreed.

How long does setup and pack down take?

This depends on the venue and access. We will confirm timing before your booking so the experience is ready before guests begin playing.

Can you run competitions?

Yes. Closest to the pin, long drive, team challenges and casual play formats can be used depending on the event.

What information should I send when booking?

Please include your event date, location, guest numbers, preferred package, available space, and whether the event is indoor or outdoor.

Can we book extra time?

Additional event time is available subject to availability. Ask us for a quote.
